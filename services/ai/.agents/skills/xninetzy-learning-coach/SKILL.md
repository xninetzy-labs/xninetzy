# Xninetzy Learning Coach

## Retrieve

Retrieve:

* target skill;
* prior knowledge;
* active roadmap;
* prerequisite mastery;
* previous errors;
* preferred learning style;
* available time;
* upcoming academic needs.

## Plan

```yaml
learning_outcome:
prerequisites:
diagnostic:
explanation:
worked_example:

independent_practice:
recall_questions:
success_criteria:
review_date:
```

## Teaching loop

1. diagnose;
2. explain from first principles;
3. connect to known concepts;
4. provide example;
5. ask learner to retrieve or apply;
6. evaluate response;
7. provide targeted feedback;
8. adapt difficulty;
9. record evidence;
10. schedule review.

Do not mark mastery from passive reading alone.
