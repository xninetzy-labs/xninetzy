## 61. Golden KRS Workflow

```text
SESSION CHECK
    ↓
BIND ACADEMIC PERIOD
    ↓
READ CURRENT KRS
    ↓
READ OFFERINGS
    ↓
READ QUOTA
    ↓
READ PREREQUISITES
    ↓
READ ELIGIBILITY
    ↓
CHECK SCHEDULE
    ↓
CHECK CREDIT LIMIT
    ↓
DRY RUN
    ↓
ACTION HASH
    ↓
EXPLICIT APPROVAL
    ↓
IMMEDIATE REVALIDATION
    ↓
CURRENT-STATE HASH CHECK
    ↓
STAGE
    ↓
READ BACK
    ↓
STAGED-STATE RECONCILIATION
    ↓
FINAL EXPLICIT APPROVAL
    ↓
SUBMIT ONCE
    ↓
PORTAL VERIFICATION
    ↓
AUDIT
```
