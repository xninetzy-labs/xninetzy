# HEBAT Assignment — Document Standard

This reference expands the DOCX body text standard, heading hierarchy, cover page, table of contents, and section title conventions. Read it when designing the visual and structural foundation of an HEBAT deliverable.

## Body text

Unless the lecturer or assignment specifies another standard, use the following baseline.

* Font: Times New Roman
* Size: 12 pt
* Color: black `#000000`
* Alignment: justified
* Line spacing: 1.5
* Paragraph spacing: consistent throughout
* Widow/orphan control: enabled where supported
* No unnecessary decorative elements
* No em dash `—`

Use normal punctuation and sentence construction instead of relying on em dashes.

## Heading hierarchy

Default hierarchy:

### H1

* 24 pt
* bold
* black
* used for major sections

### H2

* 16 pt
* bold
* black
* used for major subsections

### H3

* 13 pt
* bold
* black
* used for detailed subsections

Headings should be informative.

Prefer: `2.5 Feasibility Analysis in the Surabaya Context`

instead of: `2.5 Why This Is Easy`

Do not use unnecessary decorative prefixes such as `BAB I`, `BAB II`, etc., unless explicitly required by the lecturer.

## Cover page

When a cover page is required, use a professional full-page composition.

### Required information

The cover should normally contain:

* assignment/project title,
* subtitle when useful,
* UNAIR logo when required,
* group/team or student identity,
* lecturer name,
* study program,
* faculty,
* university,
* city,
* year.

The lecturer should be included whenever the course instructions require a lecturer field.

### Title rules

Titles should:

* describe the actual project clearly,
* avoid unnecessary punctuation,
* avoid em dashes,
* avoid overly promotional wording.

Prefer: `EcoTrack Platform for Sustainable Waste Management`

instead of: `EcoTrack — A Revolutionary Waste Solution`

### Group identity

For group assignments: `Kelompok 4`.

Do not automatically place a full member list on the cover unless required. When a member list is required, use:

1. NIM Name
2. NIM Name
3. NIM Name

Do not use icons or decorative bullets for required academic identity information.

For individual assignments: `Nama | NIM`.

### Lecturer field

Use: `Dosen: Full Name, Academic Titles`.

The lecturer name must come from authoritative course information such as:

* lecturer/course contract,
* HEBAT records,
* verified assignment materials.

Never guess the lecturer.

### Default A4 layout

Unless another format is specified:

* Paper: A4
* Top margin: 2.3 cm
* Bottom margin: 2.3 cm
* Left margin: 2.5 cm
* Right margin: 2.5 cm
* No header/footer on the cover

The cover should fit exactly on one page. When using the UNAIR logo, maintain a visually balanced vertical composition: `title → logo → identity → lecturer → academic metadata`. Verify that the logo is visually centered and that the cover does not overflow to a second page.

## Table of contents

The TOC should contain only meaningful navigational information. Do not include placeholder text such as:

> "The table of contents will be updated."

Make sure:

* headings match the document,
* numbering is consistent,
* page numbers are correct,
* indentation reflects heading levels.

## Section titles

Use informative titles. Prefer:

`2.5 In-Depth Research on the Surabaya Context and Implementation Feasibility`

instead of: `2.5 Deep Research Surabaya`

Avoid em dashes in all headings and titles.
