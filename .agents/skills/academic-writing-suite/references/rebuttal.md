# Response to reviewers (rebuttal letter)

Inputs: decision letter, reviewer comments, manuscript, and the revised text or the planned changes. Venue-specific variants: `econ/guide.md` (referee response section), `cs-systems/guide.md` ("respond to reviewers").

1. Number every point `R1.1`, `R1.2`, `R2.1`; split compound comments.
2. Per point use three parts: **Comment** (verbatim or tight paraphrase), **Response** (agree, clarify, or disagree respectfully, with evidence), **Change** (exact location: section, page, line, table or figure, plus the new text when short).
3. Triage first: must-fix (validity, methodology), should-fix, optional, out of scope. Out of scope gets a polite justification, never silence.
4. Tone: grateful, specific, non-adversarial. Write "we have clarified", never "the reviewer misunderstood".
5. Never claim a change, experiment, baseline or citation that does not exist. If it is not done, write "we plan to" or "we acknowledge this as a limitation" and mark `TODO(author)`.
6. Open with at most six bullets of major changes; close with items the editor asked for.
7. Verify before delivering: every point answered, every claimed change present in the revised text, numbers and citations consistent, changes visible via diff or tracked changes.
