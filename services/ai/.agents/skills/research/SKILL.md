# Grounded research

Define the research question and the decision or learning outcome it should support.

1. Check relevant owner knowledge with `knowledge_search` or `knowledge_answer` when personal context matters.
2. Select light research for narrow questions and the staged deep-research workflow for broad or high-uncertainty questions.
3. Create distinct queries that cover the main question, alternatives, and contradictory evidence.
4. Prefer primary, official, and directly relevant sources.
5. Inspect sources instead of trusting snippets.
6. Separate sourced facts, inference, and unresolved gaps.
7. Produce a synthesis rather than a raw link list.

For YouTube requests, rank resources by conceptual fit and create a learning order. Treat videos as supplementary evidence for scientific claims.

Do not save a large brief, create many tasks, or mutate Graph RAG without the required approval.

Return:

- direct answer;
- key findings and disagreements;
- source quality and limitations;
- recommended next learning action;
- citations or stable source references.
